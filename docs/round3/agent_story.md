# Part 1: Story of One User

### User: Ayush Sharma (Bengaluru, Karnataka)
**Monthly Income:** ₹1,00,000 | **Savings Target:** ₹15,000 (Inviolable) | **Daily Discretionary Budget:** ₹300/day

On October 2, 2026, Ayush's bank account experienced ₹82,049 of gross debits. Traditional banking applications marked him as massively overspent, shouting red warnings.

Gullak intervened autonomously:
1. When Ayush sent **₹1,500 to Rahul**, Gullak detected an ambiguous P2P transfer ($C = 0.52 < 0.88$). It asked exactly one concise question: *"Ayush, what was the ₹1,500 sent to Rahul for?"* Ayush answered: *"I lent him the money."* Gullak categorized it as a `LOAN`, logged ₹0 consumption, opened a ₹1,500 loan receivable, and committed Rahul to long-term memory.
2. When Rahul repaid **₹1,500** two weeks later, Gullak did not falsely celebrate new income; it closed the loan ledger cleanly.
3. When Ayush paid **₹4,000 for a group BBQ dinner**, he clarified his share was ₹1,000. Gullak deducted only ₹1,000 from his daily spending allowance, tracking ₹3,000 under receivables.
4. When Ayush sent a second **₹2,000 to Rahul**, Gullak checked memory and auto-inferred it as a loan without repeatedly asking. But when a later payment had narration *"Dinner split"*, Gullak flagged the contradiction and re-clarified.

**The Reconciliation Result:**
Out of ₹82,049 moved, Ayush actually only consumed **₹32,749**. The remaining ₹49,300 was fronted, lent, transferred, or invested. His ₹15,000 savings target was 100% protected. Gullak replaced financial panic with clarity.
